# Rules of Engagement

## Purpose

This project is designed for authorized phishing awareness training and security culture measurement only.

## Allowed use

- Internal awareness training with written authorization
- Safe simulated campaign analytics
- User education and reporting improvement
- Defensive metrics and executive reporting

## Prohibited use

- Credential harvesting
- Malware delivery
- Evasion of mail security controls
- Sending messages to users without authorization
- Impersonating real third parties without approval
- Testing outside owned or approved environments

## Ethical design choices

This repository intentionally avoids real email delivery logic and credential collection. It focuses on governance, measurement, and awareness outcomes rather than offensive tradecraft.

## Recommended approval workflow

1. Define campaign objective and scope.
2. Obtain written approval from security, HR, legal, and leadership where appropriate.
3. Use approved templates and training pages.
4. Measure aggregate outcomes.
5. Provide coaching and education, not punishment.
6. Document lessons learned and control improvements.
